# Communications
* Document your plans. Write them down. Don't throw away!
* Planning is a team effort
* Use code/document sharing toolst o provide access and persistence for your plans/code/etc
* Keep anything that is competition sensitive confidential. DONT leak the sauce
* Communication is enhanced by chat facilities, use them!
* Some of these tools may not transport to your competition environment, but if you use simple, open-source ones, they may
# Long Term Planning
- Successful teams have long term plans
- Plans may be for different windows, 5 years, 1 year, 6 months, 1 event
- Once goals are defined, milestones can be identified and achieved
- Use down time to develop tools and automations. Write good scripts!
- Develop contingency plans
- *Principle of time*-- plans need to be prepared and executed in a timely manner
-  New info may lead you to change plans. *principle of innovation*
